About this data

Map and place search: Google Maps. Climate: NASA POWER. Soil estimates: ISRIC SoilGrids. Crop reference ranges: FAO EcoCrop via OpenCLIM. Cultivation estimates: IFPRI MapSPAM via CGIAR.

A climate match means the compared averages fall within recorded ranges. It does not guarantee a successful harvest. Crop variety, seasonal rainfall, extreme weather, soil conditions and irrigation can change the outcome.

Climate averages describe an area; SoilGrids estimates may differ from field measurements. Satellite imagery is not live. Missing or invalid values stay unavailable.

The next-crop tool screens limited climate and pH information. It does not calculate full-season suitability, irrigation requirements, soil improvement, or a validated rotation plan.
